BiliSubStudio_v4.0.0-beta.12-RC9-WindowsTest.exe

- Van chi co MOT muc OCR duy nhat.

- Them lua chon thiet bi: Tu dong / GPU / CPU / CPU + GPU.

- CPU + GPU xu ly song song nhung subtitle van phai dung thu tu timestamp.

QUAN TRONG
Neu mot muc FAIL, dung o muc do va gui anh + dong loi/log nguyen van. Khong can tiep tuc de tranh lam mat dau vet.

0. NEN TEST SACH
----------------
- Nen dat EXE RC9 trong mot thu muc BiliSub rieng.
- Neu muon test dung first-install: khong chep Tools/OCR tu RC8 sang.
- Khong cai Python, pip, CUDA toolkit, cuDNN hay sua PATH de "giup" app.

1. KIEM TRA DANH SACH THIET BI OCR
----------------------------------
Vao OCR phu de -> xem muc "Thiet bi OCR".

May KHONG co NVIDIA GPU:
- CPU phai dung duoc.
- GPU va CPU + GPU phai bi khoa/khong cho chon.
- Tu dong phai chay CPU.

May CO NVIDIA GPU tuong thich:
- Phai thay: Tu dong / GPU / CPU / CPU + GPU.
- Dong thong tin phai hien ten NVIDIA GPU + driver.
- GPU va CPU + GPU phai cho chon.

FAIL neu app bao co GPU gia, khong nhan GPU dang co, hoac UI cho chon GPU tren may khong co GPU tuong thich.

2. CPU - CAI VA DOC THU
-----------------------
1) Chon CPU.
2) Bam "Chuan bi bo nhan dien" neu app chua san sang.
3) Lan dau app tu cai runtime vao Tools/OCR/runtime/cpu.
4) Ket qua phai la "Bo nhan dien da san sang".
5) Chon video Trung, dat ROI vao subtitle, bam "Doc thu khung hien tai".
6) Phai co text + confidence.

FAIL neu hien CMD/PowerShell lung tung, bat cai Python/pip thu cong, hoac bao Ready gia ma Doc thu khong chay.

3. GPU - BAT BUOC NEU MAY CO NVIDIA
-----------------------------------
1) Chon GPU.
2) App tu cai Tools/OCR/runtime/gpu, khong yeu cau cai CUDA/Python bang tay.
3) Cho den khi hien bo nhan dien san sang va dong trang thai "Dang dung GPU".
4) Doc thu 2-3 frame co subtitle Trung.
5) Mo Task Manager (Performance -> GPU) hoac cong cu NVIDIA de xem luc OCR co GPU activity.
6) CPU van co the dung mot phan cho FFmpeg/scan/UI; dieu can kiem tra la phan PaddleOCR inference da chay GPU.

FAIL neu:
- chon GPU nhung thuc te chi an CPU;
- bao Ready nhung GPU worker khong khoi tao;
- yeu cau cai Python/CUDA/PATH thu cong;
- app treo/crash khi chuyen CPU <-> GPU.

4. TU DONG
----------
May co NVIDIA GPU khoe:
- Chon Tu dong -> ky vong "Dang dung GPU".

May khong co GPU/driver khong dung duoc:
- Tu dong phai chay CPU va neu co fallback thi thong tin phai noi ro ly do.

Luu y: Auto duoc phep fallback CPU. Che do GPU cuong che thi KHONG duoc gia vo GPU da chay.

5. CPU + GPU - SONG SONG
------------------------
Chi test neu GPU mode o muc 3 da DONE.

1) Chon "CPU + GPU".
2) Cho bo nhan dien san sang.
3) Bat dau quet chinh xac mot doan video co nhieu subtitle va co khoang trong.
4) Theo doi CPU va GPU: ca hai nen co activity khi co nhieu OCR work.
5) Preview/list subtitle phai cap nhat binh thuong.
6) Khong duoc co cue dao timestamp, cue am, cue end < start, hoac subtitle bi dao thu tu.
7) So sanh nhanh 1 doan voi GPU-only/CPU-only: noi dung khong duoc vo ly chi vi chay Hybrid.

FAIL neu Hybrid lam app dung/treo, chi co mot worker hoat dong, hoac subtitle bi dao thu tu/timestamp.

6. FRAME KHONG CO SUBTITLE
--------------------------
Trong CPU, GPU hoac Hybrid:
- Tua den 2-3 frame khong co subtitle.
- Bam Doc thu.
- Khong nen sinh rac ro rang kieu A / W / OV.
Neu co, chup dung frame + ROI + text/confidence gui lai.

7. QUET FULL + XUAT SRT
-----------------------
1) Chon mot device mode da pass o tren.
2) Bam Bat dau quet chinh xac.
3) Preview phai tien theo backend scan.
4) Danh sach subtitle cap nhat dan.
5) Khong treo UI/backend.
6) Quet xong, Xuat SRT phai bat.
7) Mo SRT: text + timestamp phai hop ly va tang dan.

8. DUNG / TIEP TUC / CHECKPOINT
-------------------------------
1) Cho quet mot luc.
2) Bam Dung quet.
3) Dong/mo app hoac bat dau lai dung video + ROI + scan mode.
4) Phai resume tu checkpoint hop le, khong tu 00:00 neu identity van khop.
5) Viec doi CPU/GPU/Hybrid KHONG duoc lam mat checkpoint cua cung video/ROI/scan mode.

9. REGRESSION CAC PHAN CU
-------------------------
- OCR -> Chon video: native dialog mo/cancel/chon dung.
- Chinh video -> Chon video: dung.
- Codec kho: OCR va Chinh video deu co preview/fallback.
- Settings -> thu muc luu: Chon/Mo dung.
- Sidebar hien version + o dia.
- De app yen 10-15 phut roi quay lai: backend khong tu dung.
- Video download/editor khong duoc thay doi hanh vi do RC9 chi sua OCR device scheduling.
